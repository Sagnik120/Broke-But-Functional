# User guide

Broke But Functional helps you make ₹10,000 last 30 days. You can test any money decision before you make it.

## The four views

Switch views with the tab bar at the bottom of a phone, the dock on a tablet, or the sidebar on a computer. Each view has its own link, so the back button works.

### Overview

| Card                  | What it tells you                                                                              |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Safe to spend today   | Your daily allowance: what is left after upcoming bills, divided by the days remaining         |
| Financial health      | A score out of 100 with a grade: Thriving, Stable, Stretched or Critical                       |
| Financial risk        | A score out of 100 and the reasons behind it                                                   |
| Your month in numbers | Balance, spending so far, upcoming commitments, savings and the month-end forecast             |
| Impulse check         | Drag the slider to see what a one-off treat today would do. Nothing is saved                   |
| Smart moves           | Suggestions with real numbers, such as which subscription to cancel                            |
| Monthly forecast      | The solid line is what happened; the dashed line is the forecast                               |
| Month map             | All 30 days. Darker days cost more, a dot marks a bill, red marks a day your money has run out |
| Coming up             | Bills still to be charged this month                                                           |
| Savings goals         | Progress towards each goal                                                                     |
| Achievements          | Badges you have unlocked this month                                                            |

Use the arrows beside "Day 6 of 30" to move through the month. Bills are charged when their day arrives. You cannot go back past a day on which you logged something.

### What if?

1. Pick a decision, or tap a preset such as "Sneaker drop ₹2,999".
2. Fill in the details. The consequences appear straight away.
3. Read the verdict (**Go for it**, **Think twice** or **Not a good idea**) and the before/after table.
4. Choose **Apply this decision** to make it real, or change the numbers and try again.

**Throw me a curveball** draws a surprise expense so you can see whether you would cope.

| Decision              | Use it for                                                  |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Buy something         | Everyday spending. It also raises your projected daily pace |
| Unexpected expense    | A one-off. It does not change your everyday pace            |
| Start a subscription  | A recurring charge on a day of the month                    |
| Cancel a subscription | See what you would free up                                  |
| Move money to a goal  | Saving. The money leaves your spendable balance             |
| Earn extra income     | A gig, a gift or a refund                                   |

### Activity

Log an expense or income for the current day. Tick **This was unexpected** for one-offs. The list shows everything this month, and **Where it went** breaks spending down by category. Use **Remove** to undo an entry.

### Plans

- **Savings goals:** name a goal and a target, then add money to it. Removing a goal returns its money to your balance.
- **Subscriptions and commitments:** add anything with a due day. Subscriptions can be cancelled and resumed; cancelling before the due day avoids the charge.

## Good to know

- **Your data stays on this device.** There is no account and nothing is uploaded.
- **Start over:** at the bottom of the Overview, start a fresh month or reload the demo. Both ask you to confirm.
- **Dark mode:** use the sun or moon button. The app follows your system setting until you choose.
- **Offline:** after your first visit the app opens without a connection.
- **Amounts** are whole rupees, up to ₹1,00,000.
- **Keyboard:** Tab moves between controls; arrow keys move between views; Enter activates buttons.
